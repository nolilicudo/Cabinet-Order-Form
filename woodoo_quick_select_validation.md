# Woodoo Quick Select Validation

Validated in the application preview on 2026-08-06.

- The default Woodoo view opens on **Base cabinets** with Shaker / White source pricing.
- The cabinet-group controls filter the catalog into **Base cabinets**, **Wall cabinets**, **Tall & pantry**, **Vanities**, **Panels & finishing**, and **Accessories**.
- Similar source categories are presented as single illustrated family cards. Each card exposes its available size variants with the exact source SKU and Woodoo MSRP amount.
- Selected **B09FH** displayed `9"W x 34-1/2"H x 24"D`, SKU `B09FH`, and MSRP **$50.84** in the selected tray.
- Continuing to the Woodoo order sheet preserved the exact selected product and MSRP of **$50.84** without allowing a manual unit-price override.
- Dummy doors and drawer fronts are classified as **Panels & finishing** rather than Wall cabinets to keep the beginner-facing Wall group focused on cabinet bodies.
