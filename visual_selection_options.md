# Beginner-Friendly Cabinet Selection Options

The visual catalogs and source-price tables support three practical ways to make cabinet selection easier for homeowners, sales staff, and contractors who do not build cabinets every day. Each option preserves the supplier’s source-backed SKU pricing while reducing unfamiliar cabinet terminology.

| Option | Customer experience | Best fit | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Guided room-by-room builder** | The user answers a small sequence of plain-language questions: supplier, room, cabinet job, size, then finish. The app shows only relevant illustrated cabinet cards and explains common terms. | First-time users and customer-facing consultations. | Requires a few more clicks before showing the full catalog. |
| **2. Visual cabinet gallery** | The user begins with an image grid grouped as Base, Wall, Tall, Vanity, and Accessories, then filters by width, height, depth, or SKU. | Contractors or repeat users who know the cabinet category but prefer pictures over codes. | Can still feel broad when a room has many possible cabinet types. |
| **3. Layout checklist assistant** | The user selects a room layout pattern, such as a straight wall, L-shaped kitchen, island, or vanity wall. The app presents a checklist of common cabinet roles and makes each role optional. | Guided estimating and early customer budgeting conversations. | Needs more business rules for recommended quantities and layout guidance. |

## Recommended implementation: hybrid of Options 1 and 2

The recommended first release combines a short **Guided Start** with a **Visual Gallery**. A user can begin with the guided questions when they want help, skip directly to the visual gallery when they know what they need, or open an optional explainer drawer at any time. This approach is quick in a sales appointment, understandable for beginners, and avoids pretending that the application can fully design a kitchen without a measured plan.

The initial walkthrough should use the following plain-language steps: **Choose supplier**, **Choose room or cabinet job**, **Pick a cabinet shape**, **Confirm size**, **Compare finish and price**, and **Add to package**. Every card must show a cabinet drawing, SKU, plain-language description, dimensions, and supplier-derived price; it must never require a user to type a unit price.
